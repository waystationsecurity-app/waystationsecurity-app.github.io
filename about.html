The team behind WayStation.

WayStation Security builds tools for people who can't afford to get an email wrong — not for companies with a security department, for everyone else.

Why WayStation exists

It started with one email. Charles got a message claiming someone had searched his background, with an encrypted file attached — and no safe way to find out what was inside it. Opening it could have been the trap. Not opening it meant never knowing.

So he built the thing that should have existed: an inbox that opens email inside a sealed sandbox, where scripts don't run, trackers don't load, and nothing downloads on its own. WayStation is that inbox — a sandboxed email app for Android. It works with Outlook, Hotmail, MSN, Microsoft 365 and most IMAP accounts — Yahoo, AOL, iCloud, GMX and more — today; Gmail support is in the works. Pro checks every sender and link before you touch it. The app started life under the name AirLock. It became WayStation, because that is what it is: a place where mail stops and gets checked before it reaches you.

Successes, so far

WayStation went from that one suspicious email to a working app in a matter of weeks: the sealed sandbox viewer, connections to real mail providers, threat checks for spoofed senders and links that lie about where they go, and a Pro system that activates itself the moment someone subscribes — no waiting, no manual keys. Our Google Play developer identity is verified, this site and our support channels are live, and Maya, Dylan and Kit now teach scam-spotting to a growing audience, including a podcast, The Fox Files.

The setbacks, honestly

We are not on the Play Store yet. Google requires new developers to run a closed test — twelve testers, fourteen days — before a public listing, and we have not run ours yet. Some features we talk about, like safely previewing the contents of a locked attachment without opening it, are still being built; we will not claim a feature exists before it does. And a team this small moves at the speed it moves. We would rather tell you that plainly than dress it up.

Where we are going

Next: the closed test, then the public launch on Google Play. After that, the roadmap is the reason we started — safe preview for attachments, Gmail support, and more tools in the same spirit: the safety equipment ordinary people cannot currently get, priced for ordinary people. WayStation is the first of them. There will be more.
