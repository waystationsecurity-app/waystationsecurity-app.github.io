Questions

Does WayStation read or store my email on a server?

No. WayStation runs on your phone and talks directly to your email provider. There's no WayStation server holding your mail. Pro's optional online checks (when turned on) send only link addresses and attachment files to be scanned, and they are not kept.

Will WayStation delete anything from my account?

Only if you tell it to. "Delete from my account" moves an email to your provider's Trash. Closing WayStation wipes emails from your phone, not from your account, unless you turn on the optional close setting.

Why isn't it in the Google Play Store yet?

It's coming. For now you can install it directly from this site. Android will ask you to allow the install. The tutorials walk you through it.

Why can't I add Gmail?

Google requires a security review before apps can read Gmail. Gmail support is coming after that review.

Is WayStation a replacement for antivirus?

No. It's a safer way to look at email. It catches a lot, but no tool catches everything. Don't open files or sign in to links you weren't expecting.
