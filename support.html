We usually reply within 2 business days.

Email playhardllc@outlook.com. Tell us your phone model, the WayStation version (bottom of Settings), and what happened. A screenshot helps.

Quick fixes

"Sign-in failed" for iCloud or IMAP

Use an app-specific password, not your normal password. Check the server name has no spaces. WayStation only uses port 993.

Outlook says I need admin approval

Your work or school blocks new apps. Ask your IT team, or use a personal Microsoft account.

Android won't install the update

If you have an old test build, uninstall it first. Public builds can't install over test builds.

An email I need was flagged

Labels are warnings, not verdicts. Check the reasons listed in the email. If you know the sender, you can still save it to your vault.

How do I cancel Pro?

Cancel any time from the link in your purchase receipt. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
