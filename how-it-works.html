How your mail flows

No WayStation server ever holds your email. Here is the exact path a message takes.

1

Your phone talks to your provider — directly

WayStation connects straight from your phone to Outlook, Hotmail, MSN, and Microsoft 365. iCloud, Yahoo, AOL, and other IMAP accounts are coming soon. No WayStation mail server in the middle.

2

The message is scrubbed on arrival

Scripts are stripped, remote images and tracking pixels are blocked, and links are disarmed before you ever see the message.

3

It opens in a sealed viewer

The email renders in a locked-down view: JavaScript stays off, nothing downloads on its own, and links can't fire by accident.

4

Pro runs its checks on your phone

Sender authentication, look-alike domains, deceptive links, and risky attachment file types — quarantined by name. Attachment contents are never scanned or uploaded.

What the app actually pulls from each email

  • The Authentication-Results header → SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass or fail.
  • The From name and address → mismatch check (says "PayPal," isn't PayPal).
  • Every link → its real destination vs. its visible text.
  • Each attachment → file name, extension, size, and type. Never the file contents.
5

Close it and it's gone — or vaulted

Unsaved mail is wiped from your phone when you close it. Mail you keep goes into an encrypted vault on your device.
